cStar Technologies Inc. specializes in two-way wireless data communication solutions for machine-to-machine, machine-to-mobile, machine-to-human, and mobile-commerce applications. Based in Richmond Hill, Ontario, cStar’s research and development (R&D) work has boosted Canada’s technological competitiveness worldwide.

The Scientific Research and Experimental Development (SR&ED) Program’s tax incentives for qualified SR&ED expenditures have helped cStar to experiment more freely and advance their R&D; furthermore, they have allowed cStar to conduct a series of experiments that led to the invention of wireless communication solutions for their clients. The company has developed domestic and internationally-patented technologies, and has created quality jobs in Canada.

Stella Yoon, President and CEO, commends the SR&ED Program for supporting innovative businesses in Canada.

Innovation flourishes in a free environment, and this program has afforded us the flexibility we need to grow. The financial support has reduced the cost and risk of innovating and has allowed us to experiment more freely. We also received great customer service. The staff were professional, efficient, and thoughtful, which matches our experience with the program overall.

The SR&ED Program offers tax incentives to encourage Canadian businesses of all sizes and in all sectors to conduct R&D in Canada. Do you think that you might qualify? Learn more about our eligibility requirements, and see our list of services and tools to help you get started.
